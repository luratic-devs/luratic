## What it does

Repository with adapted `gtag.js` builds for sending data to GA4 from non-standard protocols such as `file:` and other environments that do not use HTTPS. It targets desktop applications and local development environments. The adaptation can be reviewed directly in `gtag.js` and `gtagqa.js`.
