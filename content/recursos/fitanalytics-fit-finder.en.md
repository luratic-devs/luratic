## What it does

Template for Fit Finder, the FitAnalytics sizing assistant. The configuration lets you choose an integration option and provide its value. The repository includes the tag file and setup documentation.

The repository includes the template file. Review its documentation, configuration and permissions before adding it to your Google Tag Manager container.
