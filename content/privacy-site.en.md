# Luratic privacy and cookies

Last updated: October 28, 2026.

This page explains the analytics used on **luratic.com**. It does not replace the specific privacy policies for Chrome extensions published by Luratic.

## What we use

Luratic uses **Google Analytics 4 (GA4)** to analyse aggregate website usage and **Microsoft Clarity** to understand interactions and sessions in order to improve the site.

Both services are loaded through Google Tag Manager. The website initializes **Google Consent Mode v2** before the container loads.

## Your choice

On a first visit, analytics storage starts with `analytics_storage: denied`. Advertising-related states (`ad_storage`, `ad_user_data` and `ad_personalization`) remain `denied`.

You can accept or reject analytics storage from the privacy notice and change your choice at any time through **Cookie settings** in the footer.

When you reject, Consent Mode keeps analytics storage denied. Compatible services may perform limited or cookieless measurement according to their behaviour and configuration.

## What we store in your browser

Luratic stores your preference in `localStorage` under the key `luratic_consent_v1`. It contains the Analytics category state, the consent mechanism version and the update timestamp.

This preference is used to remember your choice on later visits.

## Interaction data

Luratic analytics may record page views and interactions with interface elements —for example menus, buttons, links, cards and the consent notice itself— together with technical context such as the page section and language.

These events are not designed to collect data entered by users in forms or to use input-field contents as analytics parameters.

## Providers

- **Google Analytics / Google Tag Manager** — Google. See Google's privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Microsoft Clarity** — Microsoft. See Microsoft Clarity privacy information: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/clarity/setup-and-installation/privacy-disclosure

These providers may process technical and usage information according to their own policies, configuration and terms.

## Change your preferences

You can reopen the consent panel at any time through **Cookie settings** in the footer.

For privacy questions about luratic.com, contact **info@luratic.com**.
